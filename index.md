## How to play / 玩法

Tap numbers in ascending order, starting at 1. Numbers appear only after you start. Correct taps flash green; incorrect taps flash red. Numbers remain visible, so keep track of your place yourself.

Each stage saves your three fastest uninterrupted times. Pause hides the numbers. You may resume and finish, but a paused attempt does not count toward records. Sound, haptics, language, and dark mode are available in Settings.

从1开始依次点击数字。点击开始后数字才会出现；点对闪绿、点错闪红，数字不会消失。每个小关保存最快三次连续完成纪录。暂停会遮住数字，恢复后仍可过关，但该次成绩不计入纪录。设置中可切换语言、音效、震动和深色模式。

## Purchases / 购买

The first 3 chapters (15 stages) are free. A one-time non-consumable in-app purchase permanently unlocks chapters 4–12 (45 additional stages). There is no subscription. Stages still progress in order after purchase.

To restore a purchase, use the same Apple Account used to buy it, then open Settings → Restore purchases. Apple handles payments and refund requests. Deleting the app does not cancel your purchase, but local progress and records may be lost. Purchase restoration restores access, not gameplay records.

前3个大关共15小关免费，一次非消耗型内购可永久解锁第4至12大关的45小关，无订阅。购买后仍需依次过关。请使用购买时的同一 Apple 账号，在设置中点击“恢复购买”。付款和退款由 Apple 处理。删除 App 不会取消购买，但可能丢失本地进度与纪录；恢复购买仅恢复访问权限，不恢复游戏纪录。

## Privacy Policy

Updated: October 27, 2026

Orbit Seek does not require an account. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking. The app does not send your personal information or gameplay records to developer-operated servers.

Progress, completion records and dates, language, sound, haptic, and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data; backups and restoration are managed by your Apple device settings.

In-app purchases use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment and purchase-related information under its own privacy policy. The app uses verified purchase transactions to determine whether paid chapters are unlocked. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Purchase access can be restored through Apple; gameplay records are not synced through a developer account.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you voluntarily provide. We use these only to respond and provide support. You may request deletion of support correspondence, except where retention is legally required.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy statement. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to this page.

This policy will be updated here if the app's data practices change. Contact: [lu_wenguang@hotmail.com](mailto:lu_wenguang@hotmail.com).

## 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月27日
